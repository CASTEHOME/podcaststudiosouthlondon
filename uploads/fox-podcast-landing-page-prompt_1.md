# FOX PODCAST — Landing Page Build Prompt

> Copy everything below into your AI website builder (e.g. Lovable, v0, Claude, Bolt) or hand it to a designer/developer as a creative + technical brief.

---

## 1. Project Summary

Build a single-page, interactive landing page for **FOX PODCAST**, a London-based podcast production studio. FOX PODCAST films and edits video podcasts to a **Netflix-level production standard**, offering both studio sessions and on-location shoots (offices, events, live sessions). The site must have almost 70 five-star Google reviews as a trust signal and should **sell the outcome — the success and quality of the podcasts we've produced — not the technical process**. Visitors should come away thinking "these are the people whose work makes podcasts look and feel like a Netflix show," not "these are camera operators."

The primary conversion goal is to get visitors to **book a session directly through an interactive calendar** embedded on the page (e.g. Calendly, Cal.com, or a custom date-picker widget).

---

## 2. Target Audience

Design and write copy for three overlapping audiences, in this priority order:

1. **Businesses & brands** — companies wanting a branded podcast for marketing, thought leadership, or internal content.
2. **Entrepreneurs & creators** — individual hosts, coaches, consultants, and influencers who want their personal podcast to look premium.
3. **Corporate & events clients** — companies who want FOX PODCAST to travel on-location to an office or live event and set up a full podcast recording session there.

The copy should speak to decision-makers who care about **how the finished product will look and perform**, not the gear or workflow used to make it.

---

## 3. Brand Voice & Positioning

- Confident, premium, cinematic — never salesy or gimmicky.
- Position FOX PODCAST as the studio that turns a podcast into a **binge-worthy show**, not just a recorded conversation.
- Lead every section with outcomes: growth, views, polish, guest experience, brand credibility — not equipment lists or editing jargon.
- London-rooted but not parochial — "London's premium podcast studio," with a nod to servicing high-profile brands and individuals.
- Tone: assured, a little aspirational, tightly written. Short punchy headlines, no fluff paragraphs.

---

## 4. Visual Art Direction — "Netflix Standard"

Design this as a **dark, cinematic, premium theme** built from scratch (no existing brand assets yet — this design pass defines the brand look):

- **Palette:** near-black background (#0A0A0A – #121212), off-white/cream text, one bold accent color (suggest a warm amber/gold or a saturated red — evoke a "studio red light on air" feel) used sparingly for CTAs and highlights.
- **Typography:** a tight, modern grotesk or condensed sans for headlines (bold, large, cinematic — think streaming-platform title cards), paired with a clean readable sans for body copy.
- **Imagery:** full-bleed video/photo backgrounds, subtle gradient overlays for text legibility, grain/vignette texture for a "film" feel. Treat every section like a movie poster or streaming platform title card, not a corporate brochure.
- **Motion:** smooth scroll-triggered fade/slide-ins, subtle parallax on hero media, autoplay-muted video loops in hero and portfolio cards, hover states that feel tactile (scale/glow on cards and buttons).
- **Layout influence:** borrow directly from Netflix/Apple TV UI conventions — horizontal-scrolling "shelves" of featured podcast episodes, large poster-style thumbnails, bold title treatment over video stills.

---

## 5. Site Structure & Section-by-Section Content

Build this as a single scrolling landing page with a sticky/minimal nav. Sections in order:

### A. Navigation (sticky, transparent-to-solid on scroll)
Logo (FOX PODCAST wordmark), links to Work / Services / On-Location / Reviews / FAQ, and a persistent "Book a Session" CTA button.

### B. Hero Section
- Full-screen looping background video reel of best podcast clips (fast cuts, cinematic grade).
- Headline built around the outcome, not the service — e.g. *"Podcasts filmed and edited to a Netflix standard."*
- Subheadline naming London studio + on-location availability.
- Primary CTA button: **"Book Your Session"** (scrolls to / opens the interactive calendar).
- Secondary CTA: **"See Our Work"** (scrolls to portfolio).
- Small trust strip directly under the fold: Google review stars + "70+ 5-star reviews" badge (linked to actual Google review page).

### C. Social Proof Bar
A slim, high-contrast strip with: star rating graphic, "Rated 5.0 from 70+ reviews on Google," and logos/names of notable clients or shows filmed (placeholder logos until real ones are supplied — see Section 8).

### D. "The Work" / Success Showcase (this is the heart of the page)
This section must dominate the page — it is what sells FOX PODCAST, not the technical service list.

- Netflix-style horizontal-scrolling shelf of **podcast "posters"** — each card is a cinematic still/thumbnail from a filmed podcast, with hover-to-preview video.
- Under or beside the shelf, feature 3–4 **case-study style highlights**: podcast name, a one-line result/achievement (e.g. views gained, audience growth, guest caliber, "now a top 10 UK business podcast"), and an embedded video reel.
- Each card should feel like clicking into a show's title card on a streaming platform — big image, bold title, short tagline, subtle metadata (episode count, genre/category).
- Include a "Play Showreel" button that opens a full studio highlight reel (modal or lightbox video player).

### E. Services (kept secondary to the success showcase, but present)
Two clear offerings, framed around outcomes:

1. **Studio Production (London)** — "Record in our London studio, come out with a show that looks like it belongs on Netflix." Cover: multi-camera setup, professional lighting/sound, full edit, delivery-ready episodes — described in benefit language, not spec-sheet language.
2. **On-Location Podcasting** — "We bring the studio to you." Cover: filming at offices, corporate events, live sessions; full mobile setup with the same production quality as the studio; ideal for corporate teams and event organizers who want a podcast moment on-site.

Use short benefit-driven copy blocks or icon + text cards, not long technical descriptions.

### F. Studio Showcase
A visual section (photo gallery or short video walkthrough) of the actual London studio — set design, lighting rig, camera setup — to build credibility and "wow" without being overly technical. Caption it around atmosphere ("Built for guests to feel like they're on a real show set") rather than gear specs.

### G. Testimonials / Reviews
- Pull directly from the ~70 Google 5-star reviews — feature 4–6 strong quotes in a carousel or grid, each with reviewer name/company and a live link to the full Google review profile.
- Include an embedded Google Reviews widget or badge if possible for real-time credibility.

### H. Process (brief, confidence-building, not technical)
A simple 3–4 step visual timeline: e.g. *Book → Plan → Film (studio or on-location) → Receive your Netflix-standard episodes.* Keep each step to one short line.

### I. On-Location Spotlight
A dedicated mini-section (if not fully covered in Services) showing FOX PODCAST's mobile setup in action at an office/event — photos + a short line about how it works logistically (travel radius from London, setup time, equipment brought).

### J. Booking Section — Interactive Calendar
This is the primary conversion point of the page:

- Embed a live interactive calendar/date-picker (Calendly, Cal.com, or custom-built) directly on the page — not just a "contact us" form.
- Let visitors choose session type (Studio / On-Location), pick an available date/time, and submit their details in one flow.
- Section headline should reinforce outcome + urgency, e.g. *"Your podcast could look like this. Pick a date and let's film it."*
- Make this CTA repeat at least 2–3 times across the page (hero, after showcase, footer) so it's always reachable.

### K. FAQ
Address practical concerns briefly: turnaround time, what's included, travel radius for on-location, pricing approach (e.g. "custom quotes based on your project — book a call to discuss"), equipment/crew, episode ownership/rights.

### L. Footer
London studio address/area, contact email/phone, social links (Instagram/YouTube/LinkedIn/TikTok — wherever podcast clips live), Google review link, booking CTA repeated, copyright.

## 6. Interactivity & Functional Requirements

- **Interactive booking calendar** embedded inline (not just an external link) — this is the single most important interactive element on the page.
- Horizontal-scroll / swipeable "shelf" component for the podcast showcase (touch-friendly on mobile).
- Hover-to-preview video on portfolio cards (desktop); tap-to-play on mobile.
- Scroll-triggered animations (fade/slide) for section reveals — subtle, not distracting.
- Sticky nav with active-section highlighting.
- Modal/lightbox video player for the full showreel.
- Testimonial carousel (auto-rotating, swipeable).
- Smooth-scroll anchor links from nav and CTA buttons.

---

## 7. Technical Requirements

- Fully responsive (mobile-first breakpoints; the horizontal shelves and calendar embed must work well on mobile).
- Fast-loading: compress/optimize video backgrounds, lazy-load below-the-fold video and images.
- SEO basics: descriptive title/meta description targeting "video podcast studio London," "podcast filming and editing London," "corporate podcast production London"; semantic heading structure; alt text on all images.
- Accessibility: sufficient contrast against the dark theme, captions/transcripts available on showreel video, keyboard-navigable calendar and nav.
- Structured data / Google Business integration where possible to reinforce the review count and rating in search results.
- Analytics + conversion tracking on the booking calendar CTA.
